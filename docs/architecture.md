# Architecture

The stack is organized around protocol adapters, normalized market data, Pyth-aware risk tooling, liquidation dry-run and replay, execution reliability analytics, and dashboard/API surfaces.

Initial public OSS scope: adapter standard, sample adapters, schemas, SDK interfaces, dry-run/replay fixtures, and public dashboard subset.

Commercial scope may include premium APIs, managed integrations, private reliability analytics, and future controlled execution after separate approval.
