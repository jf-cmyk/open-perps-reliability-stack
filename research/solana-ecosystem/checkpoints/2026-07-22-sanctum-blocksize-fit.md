# Sanctum And Blocksize Fit

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Finding

Sanctum documents a staking-as-a-service platform with more than 1,361 bespoke LSTs, custom validator support, optional operator revenue sharing, shared liquidity, and transaction-delivery infrastructure. Its documented package gates shared Infinity liquidity at 1,000 SOL of external stake and Infinity reallocation eligibility at 10,000 SOL. DefiLlama's accessed snapshot reported approximately $1.160B for the Sanctum Validator LSTs Solana TVL row.

## Blocksize Opportunity

A branded Blocksize LST could pair validator economics with performance, stake-retention, liquidity, and delivery-reliability reporting. This is a commercial-adjacent partnership opportunity, not part of the core OPRS grant unless limited to open read-only reliability interfaces.

## Qualification Gaps

Public evidence does not establish Blocksize's eligible external stake, user demand, fee economics, integration terms, liquidity risk, concentration effects, or a partnership. DefiLlama protocol rows may overlap and are not proof of unique assets or users.
