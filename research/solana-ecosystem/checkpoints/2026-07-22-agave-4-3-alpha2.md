# Agave 4.3 Alpha 2

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Finding

Anza published Agave `v4.3.0-alpha.2` on July 21. The comparison with alpha.1 contains 105 commits, including Votor voting-service and rewards work and multiple BLS certificate-verification changes. The release is marked prerelease and has no substantive release notes. Solana Foundation continues to list VAT as pending on mainnet.

## Implication

This is evidence of active Alpenglow client development, not production readiness. Blocksize monitoring should distinguish alpha availability from testnet recommendation, mainnet recommendation, validator adoption, and feature activation.

## Claim Boundary

Do not recommend production deployment or claim Blocksize adoption, stability, performance, audit completion, VAT activation, or Alpenglow activation from this release.
