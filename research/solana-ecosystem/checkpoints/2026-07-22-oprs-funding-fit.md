# OPRS Foundation Funding Fit

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Finding

Solana Foundation reviews funding applications on a rolling basis. Its standard grants target public goods, while convertible grants can support public goods with commercial components. Evaluation emphasizes open or freely usable outputs, Solana-specific necessity, clear budgets, and measurable milestones. The Open Perps call separately welcomes complementary infrastructure and notes that grants may be available.

## OPRS Positioning

The standard-grant package should fund the open reliability layer: canonical adapters, deterministic read-only replay, reason codes, public schemas, and reproducible proof packs. Blocksize managed services, paid data, hosting, and enterprise support should remain outside that funded scope unless the application explicitly pursues the convertible-grant path.

## Claim Boundary

These pages establish application paths and evaluation criteria, not OPRS eligibility, selection, funding, or award timing.
