# Frontier Traders As An OPRS Channel

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Verified

Solana Foundation launched Frontier Traders on June 17, 2026 as a network-level program for professional onchain traders. The Foundation says its founding venues account for more than 90% of Solana spot and perpetual trading activity and names Jupiter and Phoenix among them. Qualification uses rolling 30-day volume and open interest. The current program surface says VIP 3 and above receive priority RPC through Triton and Helius, alongside account support, early product access, introductions, and structured roadmap input.

Primary sources:

- https://solana.com/vi/news/introducing-frontier-traders
- https://www.frontiertraders.com/

## Blocksize And OPRS Implication

Frontier Traders is a plausible opt-in design-partner and distribution channel for read-only OPRS proof packs. A bounded pilot could measure venue and RPC reliability, oracle and mark/index state, transaction landing, liquidation reconstruction, and incident evidence for professional participants without handling keys, orders, or capital. The channel is more valuable than a generic trader campaign because it already connects cross-venue activity, infrastructure benefits, and market-structure feedback.

Current campaign pages may be spot-only even though the broader program includes perpetual venues.
