# Alpenglow Schedule Revision

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Finding

The current Solana Foundation VAT page says Alpenglow activation with Agave 4.3 is expected in October 2026. The official upgrades index still labels Alpenglow under development and VAT pending on mainnet. This conflicts with or revises the Foundation's July 2 changelog target of August for Alpenswitch mainnet.

## Operating Implication

Treat August as the prior target and October as the latest official expectation, not a guarantee. Readiness reporting must distinguish BLS registration, VAT activation, Agave 4.3 client availability, Votor rollout, and the final consensus switch. Mainnet activation remains unverified.

## Open Question

Does October replace August for the same Alpenswitch milestone, or do the two pages describe different stages of the rollout?
