# MoneyGram SDP Signal

Observed: 2026-07-22

## Finding

Solana Foundation and MoneyGram state that MoneyGram joined Solana Developer Platform and became an active Solana validator on June 22, 2026. MoneyGram reports more than 60 million active customers across 200 countries and territories and nearly half a million retail locations, with blockchain and stablecoins integrated into treasury, product development, and payments operations.
